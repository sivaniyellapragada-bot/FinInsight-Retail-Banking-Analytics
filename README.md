# Theory
## Business Intelligence In Retail Banking
Business Intelligence refers to the processes and technologies used to collect,organize,analyze,and visualize business data to support informed decison-making.
In the banking sector,BI enables orgnazations to examine large volumes to customer and financial information and identify patterns that may be immediately visible in raw datasets.
Retail banking generates data from several operating areas,including customer accounts,loan applications,credit evaluations,deposits,withdrawals,and branch activiies.
Analysing these sources together can provide a broader understanding of financial performance and customer behaviour
## Customer Credit Risk
Consumer credit risk represents the possibility that a borrower may fail to meet their financial obligations.
Credit scores,income levels,exsisting debt obligations,loan amounts and costumer characteristics are commonly considered when evaluating credit risk.
A key indicator is used in this  project is the default rate,which represents the proportion of loans or customers classified as being in default.
Another important measure is the Debt-to-income(DTI)ratio, which compares a customer;'s debt obligations with their income.
A higher DTI may indicate greater financial pressure and can be an important factor in credit-risk analysis.
## Loan Portofolio Analysis
Loan portofolio analysis involves examining the distribution and the performance of different examining the distribution and performance of different lending products.
Products such as home loans,personal loans,auto loans,and credit cards maby be different levels of exposure and risk.
Analyzing loan amount,application volume,credit characteristics,interest rates, and default behavior allows management to understand the composition and performance of the lending portfoilo.
## Branch & Regional Performance
Branch-level analysis provides insight into how different banking locations contribute to loan activity,deposits,withdrawals,and credit performance.
Comparing branches and regions can help identify trends,fluctuations,and potential seasonal patterns.
Net transaction value can be used as an analytical measure to compare deposit inflows against withdrawal outflows over a selected period.
## Excel Based Business Intelligence
Microsoft Excel provides a range of tools for implementing Business Intelligence workflows including structured tables,formulas,PivotTables,PivotCharts,Slicers,Timelines,and interactive visualizations.
In FinInsights,these capabilities are combined to transform prepared banking data into analytical summaries and an interactive dashboard that allows users to explore finacial and operational information from multiple perspective.
## Problem Statement
Modern retail banking institutions generate large volumes of customer,loan,transaction,and branch-level data through thier daily operations.
This information can provide valuable insights into financial performance and customer bevaiour;however,when data is maintained across separate datasets or analyzed without a unified framework,obtaininga a comprehensive view of banking operations become diffcult.
A major analytical requirement is the identification of credit risk within the loan portfolio.
Banking management needs to understand which customer segments,income groups,credit-score categories,loan products,and regions are associated with higher default rates and greater loan exposure.
Another important requirement is the evaluation of branch and product performance.
Differences in loan volumes,default levels,deposits,withdrawals,and transaction activity can make it difficult to determine which branches or lending products require closer management attention.
In addition,changes in customer deposit and withdrawal behaviour over time can influence the understanding of liquidity patterns.
Without effective trend analysis and visualization,seasonal fluctuations and significant changes in transaction activity may be difficult to identify.
Therefore,there is a need for a consolidated Business Intelligence solution that can organize retail banking data,performe structured and risk analysis,and present key findings through an interactive and accessible dashboard.
FInInsight addresses this requirement by developing an Excel-based analytical and visualization framework that integrates relevant banking data and provides a unified view of customer credit risk,loan portfolio performance,branch operations,and transaction trends.
